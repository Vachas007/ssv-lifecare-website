# 🚨 Pharmaceutical Compliance & Legal Guide

## ⚖️ Important: Medical & Legal Disclaimer

This website is for SSV LIFECARE, a pharmaceutical company. The following guidelines must be strictly followed:

## 🏥 Medical Claims Policy

### ✅ ALLOWED Claims (with proper documentation)

- Product composition (exact ingredients)
- Dosage form (Tablet, Injection, etc.)
- Therapeutic category (general classification)
- Instructions: "Use only under medical supervision"
- "As directed by healthcare professional"

### ❌ PROHIBITED Claims (without documentation)

- "100% safe" or "completely safe"
- "Guaranteed cure"
- "No side effects"
- "Best medicine" or "Most effective"
- "Clinically proven" (without supporting studies)
- "FDA approved" (without certificate)
- "GMP certified" (without certificate)
- "Number 1 product"
- "Permanent solution"
- Specific dosage recommendations
- Specific treatment protocols

## 🏢 Regulatory Compliance

### Product Registration

Ensure all products are:
- [ ] DCGI registered (India)
- [ ] Properly licensed
- [ ] Legally manufactured
- [ ] Within approved indications

### Manufacturing Compliance

- [ ] GMP certified (if claimed)
- [ ] Quality control documented
- [ ] Safety standards met
- [ ] Regulatory audits passed

### Advertising Guidelines

- No misleading claims
- All information verified
- Professional language used
- Educational focus only
- Business opportunity focus (not patient-directed)

## 🔍 Prescription Product Management

For products requiring prescriptions:

1. **Clear Labeling**
   - Mark as "Prescription Only"
   - Display prescription warning
   - No direct consumer sales

2. **Information Only**
   - Provide composition only
   - Healthcare professional education
   - No patient treatment advice

3. **Distribution**
   - Licensed pharmacies only
   - Medical professionals only
   - Proper documentation
   - No over-the-counter sales

## 📝 Content Verification Process

Before publishing ANY medical/health information:

1. ✅ Verify with official product documentation
2. ✅ Check approved indications only
3. ✅ Ensure no unsubstantiated claims
4. ✅ Add appropriate disclaimers
5. ✅ Have legal review
6. ✅ Update regularly

## ⚠️ What NOT to Do

❌ Make unverified medical claims
❌ Promise cures or permanent solutions
❌ Recommend specific dosages
❌ Suggest diagnosis or treatment
❌ Claim superiority without data
❌ Use fake testimonials
❌ Hide side effects or contraindications
❌ Claim certifications you don't have
❌ Target patients directly for prescription drugs
❌ Pressure users to purchase
